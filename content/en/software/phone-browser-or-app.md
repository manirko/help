Playing on the screen and receiving notes from a USB device are different features. If touching the screen makes sound, that does not prove the browser sees your device.

## What do I want to do?

| Your goal | Where to start |
|---|---|
| Try on-screen play | Open [Play](https://play.playtronica.com) and play on the screen. No USB device is needed. |
| Play a device through a phone or tablet | Choose a MIDI app from [Mobile apps](/software/mobile/) and check its requirements for your app and system versions. |
| Change device settings | Start on a computer with your device guide. A working music app on a phone does not prove that web settings are available. |
| Update firmware | Use a computer and instructions confirmed for your board. A phone failing to see the device is not a reason to update. |

Our help does not describe mobile web synths as a generally tested route for USB devices. Check the particular phone, operating system, browser, and device together.

## How do I check a mobile app connection?

Check whether your phone has Lightning or USB-C. You need a data cable and a compatible USB MIDI connection. For Android, check the phone documentation for USB Host/OTG support.

Open your music app. If it offers a MIDI input selector, select the device. Load a software instrument and check for incoming notes.

If the on-screen instrument plays but the device is missing from the inputs, changing sounds will not fix recognition. If the device appears without notes, check its playing instructions.

Before buying an adapter, check its purpose, data support, and compatibility with your system. The connector name alone is not enough.

## It plays in an app, but Settings does not work

That is a useful result. Record the app, operating system, and browser. To change settings, use a computer as described in your device guide. Do not update firmware to obtain a browser feature that is unavailable.
