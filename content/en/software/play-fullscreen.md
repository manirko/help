Open [Play](https://play.playtronica.com) and select **Full screen** beside the gear icon, if your version shows it there. Play fills the screen when your browser supports this mode. If you do not see the separate icon, use the menu route below.

To leave, select the same icon, now labelled **Exit full screen**. On a computer, you can also use the browser's exit control, usually **Esc**.

## There is no separate icon on my computer

In the earlier desktop interface, the button is inside the menu:

1. Select the **Help & MIDI** gear.
2. Open the **MIDI** tab.
3. Expand **Full screen & install** and select **Full screen**.

If neither route shows a button, your browser may not offer full-screen mode to the page. Continue playing in the normal window. A missing button alone does not indicate a Playtron fault.

## Do I need Full screen to use Playtron?

No. Full screen changes how the page is displayed. You do not need it to start playing.

For plugging in and playing, follow the [Playtron Quick start](/devices/playtron/#quick-start).
