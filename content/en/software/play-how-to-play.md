**How to play** is a short guide for your first Playtron connection in Play. You can close it with the cross and return to playing. You do not need to repeat it every time.

## How do I open the guide again?

Select the **Help & MIDI** gear, then choose **How to play**. The neighbouring **MIDI** tab contains device selection and connection controls.

## What does the guide show?

1. **USB.** Plug Playtron in with a data cable. Allow MIDI access if your browser asks.
2. **Clip · hold · touch.** Clip a wire between a Playtron note pin and a conductive object. Hold the gold **Ground** corner with one hand. Touch the object with the other hand, then release it.
3. **Did you hear it?** Touch the object again and confirm whether you hear the selected sound.

For more freedom, clip a separate wire to Ground and hold its metal end. See the [Playtron guide](/devices/playtron/) for the complete setup. The [Ground guide](/sound/grounding/) explains the contact.

A detected touch does not prove that you hear sound. If you hear nothing, choose **No sound** or **Check sound**. Check volume and the selected audio output first.

To try sounds without a device, choose **Play on screen / keyboard**.

## Why did the guide appear again?

Play remembers the guide in this site's data in this browser. The reminder is not stored in Playtron.

The guide may return in another browser, after clearing site data, or when saving site data is blocked. The main site and beta remember it separately.

If you follow a link directly to a sound pack, the guide may not open automatically. You can still open it through the gear.

If you have finished the guide but hear nothing, follow [No sound](/troubleshooting/no-sound/).
