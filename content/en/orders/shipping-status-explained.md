Check the latest event on the carrier's tracking page. A shipping email and tracking number alone do not confirm that the carrier has received your package.

## What does the status mean?

Carriers use different labels. Read the event description, date, and location.

| What you see | What it tells you |
|---|---|
| **Label created** | Shipment details have been registered. For example, UPS does not treat this status as confirmation that it has received the package. |
| **Accepted by carrier** | There is an acceptance event. Look for later events in the carrier's history. |
| **In transit** | The carrier reports movement through its network. Read the latest event as well as the overall status. |
| **Customs clearance** | Read the details to see whether you need to act. Do not pay through an unfamiliar link. |
| **Ready for collection** | Check the collection address and storage deadline on the carrier's page. |
| **Delivered** | The carrier has recorded delivery. If you do not have the package, check the recorded location and email us. |

A missing scan alone does not show whether a package is lost or already with the carrier.

## The email says shipped, but tracking only says label created

Open the event history from your shipping email. If it does not confirm carrier acceptance, email us using the details below. This matters especially if the delivery estimate shown at checkout or in tracking has passed.

## My tracking number cannot be found

Find the Playtronica shipping email and check your spam folder. If it is missing, email us your order number and the email address used at checkout. Do not post these details in a community group.
