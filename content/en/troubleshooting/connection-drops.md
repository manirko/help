When the sound stops, check whether the device disappears from your system or remains listed without sending notes. This helps you choose the next step. Leave the firmware unchanged while you check.

## Did the device disappear, or only the sound?

Save a screenshot of the MIDI device list. On a Mac, use **Audio MIDI Setup → Window → Show MIDI Studio**. On Windows, check **Device Manager** and your music app's input list.

| What happens after the interruption | What to check next |
|---|---|
| The device disappears from the system | USB cable, connector, adapter, and connection route. |
| The device remains listed, but incoming notes stop | Selected MIDI input, other open apps, and contacts described in your device guide. |
| Incoming notes continue | Software instrument, track, volume, and audio output. |

If notes continue, follow [No sound](/troubleshooting/no-sound/).

## How can I repeat the check?

Close other MIDI apps and browser tabs. Leave only the device you are checking plugged in. Open the app where the interruption happened.

Repeat the same way of playing. Note whether sound stops during play, after inactivity, or when another device is plugged in. This is an observation, not a diagnosis.

Then change one condition at a time:

1. Try another cable you know carries data.
2. Try another USB connector on the computer.
3. Record whether you use an adapter or USB hub.
4. If another computer is available, compare the result and record both operating systems.

Do not apply “remove the hub” to every device revision. Older Micro-USB TouchMe and Playtron devices may need a different route on a newer Mac. Check [Device will not connect](/troubleshooting/wont-connect/) first.

> ⚠️ If the connector is damaged, stop and photograph it. Do not use tools to restore contact.

## What should I email support?

[Email support@playtronica.com](mailto:support@playtronica.com) with the subject `Connection drops #[your order number]`. Include:

- Your device model and a photo of its USB connector.
- Your operating system, browser, or music app.
- The cable, adapter, and hub, if used.
- What remained visible after the interruption: device entry, incoming notes, and indicators.
- What happened before the interruption and what restored sound.
- Results with another cable or computer, if checked.

Attach a short video showing before and after the interruption. Repeated resets are not a substitute for identifying which stage stops working.
